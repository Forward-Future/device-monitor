Usage

Power is estimated "at the wall". NVIDIA GPU power is measured, and CPU power is measured where the device has a sensor (LibreHardwareMonitor on Windows, NVIDIA Grace or RAPL on Linux). Otherwise it's estimated from load. Motherboard, memory, drives, fans and power-supply losses are always estimated. Expect totals within about 20% of a plug-in power meter. Energy is only counted while the hub is running and the device is reporting.
